  This policy explains how Tais Tattoos handles personal data collected through this website.

  ## Who is responsible

  The person responsible for your data is Tais, an independent tattoo artist based in Zrenjanin, Serbia. You can contact her at [tais.tattoos.contact@gmail.com](mailto:tais.tattoos.contact@gmail.com).

  ## What we collect and why

  **Contact form.** When you send the contact form we receive your name, your email address, and the answers you choose to give (for example your phone or WhatsApp number, tattoo placement, size, colours and your message). We use them only to reply to your inquiry and to plan a possible appointment.

  **Shop orders.** When you place an order we receive your name, phone number, email address, delivery address (street, city and postal code), any note you add, and the items you ordered. We use them to confirm, prepare and deliver your order, and we send you a confirmation email.

  For both forms we also record the language of the page you used, so we can answer you in it.

  ## Legal basis

  We process contact form data because you asked us to contact you and gave your consent when sending the form. We process order data because it is needed to fulfil the order you placed. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

  ## Spam and abuse protection

  To keep bots out, the forms use Cloudflare Turnstile. It checks technical information from your browser, such as your IP address, to tell humans from bots. This is done by Cloudflare under its own privacy policy.

  To stop the forms from being flooded, we also count how often they are sent. For this we store only a scrambled one-way code made from your IP address (and, for orders, from your email address), never the address itself. These counters are kept by Upstash and are deleted automatically within 24 hours.

  ## Who receives your data

  - Vercel, which hosts this website.
  - Cloudflare, which provides spam protection.
  - Upstash, which stores the temporary counters described above.
  - Google (Gmail), which delivers form messages and orders to Tais and sends your order confirmation.

  These providers may process data outside Serbia and the EU.

  ## How long we keep it

  - Inquiries: only as long as needed to handle them, and deleted within 12 months unless we are in an ongoing working relationship.
  - Orders: as long as needed to deliver the order and handle any complaint, and longer only where the law requires it.
  - Spam protection counters: deleted automatically within 24 hours.

  ## Your rights

  You can ask to see your data, get a copy of it, correct or delete it, limit or object to its use, or withdraw your consent. Write to [tais.tattoos.contact@gmail.com](mailto:tais.tattoos.contact@gmail.com). You also have the right to complain to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection in Serbia, or to the data protection authority in your own country.

  ## Cookies

  This website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from this website itself, so your browser does not contact Google to display them.
