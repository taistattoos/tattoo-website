  This policy explains how Tais Tattoos handles personal data collected through this website.

  ## Who is responsible

  The person responsible for your data is Tais, a freelance tattoo artist. You can contact her using the email address shown at the bottom of this page.

  ## What we collect and why

  When you send the contact form we receive your name, your email address, and the answers you choose to give (for example your phone or WhatsApp number, tattoo placement, size, colours and your message). We use them only to reply to your inquiry and to plan a possible appointment.

  We also record the language of the page you used, so we can answer you in it.

  ## Legal basis

  We process this data because you asked us to contact you and by sending the form you consent to it. You can withdraw consent at any time.

  ## Spam protection

  To keep bots out, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile. It checks technical information from your browser, such as your IP address, to tell humans from bots. This is done by Cloudflare under its own privacy policy.

  ## Who receives your data

  - Vercel, which hosts this website.
  - Cloudflare, which provides spam protection.
  - Google (Gmail), which delivers the form message to Tais.

  These providers may process data outside Serbia and the EU.

  ## How long we keep it

  We keep your messages only as long as needed to handle your inquiry, and delete them within 12 months unless we are in an ongoing working relationship.

  ## Your rights

  You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your data, to limit its use, or to withdraw your consent. Write to the email address below. You also have the right to complain to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection in Serbia, or to the data protection authority in your own country.

  ## Cookies

  This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.
